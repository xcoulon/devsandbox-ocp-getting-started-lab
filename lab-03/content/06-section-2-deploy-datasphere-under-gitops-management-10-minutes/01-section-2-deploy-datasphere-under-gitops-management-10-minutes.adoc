= Section 2: Deploy DataSphere under GitOps management (10 minutes)
:source-highlighter: highlightjs

Rather than running `oc` commands, you're going to point ArgoCD at your Gitea
repository and let it do the deployment. The Helm chart you used in Module 1's
`helm install` step lives in your `datasphere-gitops` repository in Gitea -- ArgoCD
just needs to know where to find it.
