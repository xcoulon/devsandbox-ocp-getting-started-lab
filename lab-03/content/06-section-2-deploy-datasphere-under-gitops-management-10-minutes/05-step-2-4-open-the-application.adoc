= Step 2.4: Open the application
:source-highlighter: highlightjs

Open the DataSphere app that ArgoCD just deployed:

link:https://datasphere-ui-{user}-gitops.{openshift_cluster_ingress_domain}/[https://datasphere-ui-{user}-gitops.{openshift_cluster_ingress_domain}/, window=_blank]

This looks identical to the DataSphere you opened in Step 0.1 -- and that's the point.
It's the same application, but this is a *separate, independent deployment* in `{user}-gitops`,
managed entirely by ArgoCD. The pre-deployed instance in `{user}-datasphere` was installed
by automation; this one was deployed from your Git repository. From here on, every change
to this deployment goes through Git -- no `oc` commands, no Helm CLI.
